ROOMS

Pre-rendered 3D (Blender, Cycles), like Sister Location, with a film-grade pass. A poor provincial Filipino house: hollow blocks and an open GI roof, tabla, sawali, cracked cement, kulambo, plastic wardrobes, monobloc chairs, Huling Hapunan on the wall. Tap an image for full size.

ENTITIES

Each one comes from Filipino folklore and keeps its game name and rules.
